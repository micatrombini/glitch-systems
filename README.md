# Experimental Glitch Systems

**Experimental Glitch Systems** is my artistic research into glitch as a generative force within mathematical structures, algorithms, and computational systems. I explore how relations, rules, and operations produce instability, discontinuity, and unexpected behaviours, and how these dynamics can become material for sonic experimentation.

I approach code as both a compositional medium and a space for critical inquiry. By designing and modifying computational systems, I investigate how changes in parameters, operational rules, and internal relations transform their behaviour. Error, deviation, and structural rupture become ways of probing the limits of a system and discovering possibilities that emerge from within its own logic.

My research is also informed by the study of living systems, their forms of organisation, interactions, and transformations. I explore connections between computational processes and biological dynamics, considering how variability, feedback, and the emergence of new behaviours can inform and open up other forms of sonic experimentation.

Each experiment begins with a specific mathematical or computational structure and develops through the interplay of code, sound, and observation. I document algorithms, methods, and results as part of a research practice that connects artistic experimentation with computational thinking. The project moves across programming languages, technologies, and forms of sonic expression.

## Lines of Experimentation

- Glitch as a generative force within computational systems
- Nonlinear dynamics, instability, and structural transformation
- Algorithms, operational rules, and sonic composition
- Living systems, emergence, and interactions between processes
- Computational structures and experimental sound
